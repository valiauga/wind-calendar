Privacy policy

Wind Calendar is a kitesurfing forecast project maintained by Lukas Valiauga. It publishes forecast events to a small set of public, per-spot Google calendars.

Google Calendar access

Only the calendar owner authorizes the app. The app requests calendar.app.created permission to create its wind calendars and read, create, update and delete events on those calendars. It does not request access to your personal, work or family calendars, Gmail or contacts.

Subscribing to a calendar is a normal Google Calendar action ("add this calendar") — it never asks you to sign in to this site or grants this app any access to your account.

Storage and service providers

Google stores the calendars and their events. The owner's authorization configuration is stored locally with owner-only file permissions and, for automatic syncing, as an encrypted Render environment variable. Render hosts the website and runs the hourly sync job. Open-Meteo receives forecast requests for the predefined spots. KNMI's public observations API is used server-side, via an API key, to nudge near-term forecasts toward current conditions at spots with a nearby weather station; no visitor data is sent to KNMI.

The site includes no advertising or visitor analytics.

Use and sharing

Google data is used only to publish and maintain these calendars. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Wind Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and controls

Obsolete future forecast events are removed during successful syncs; past events remain until the owner deletes them. Unsubscribe at any time in your own calendar app — that only removes your subscription, it doesn't affect the shared calendar.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the project's issue tracker, or the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
