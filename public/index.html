Wind Calendar

Kitesurfing wind windows for Dutch spots, pushed straight into a Google Calendar you subscribe to. No account, no app, no sign-in — add a whole region, or check just the spots you care about for a custom mix.

How it works

Each region and each individual spot has its own public calendar. Add to Google Calendar adds one directly if you use Google Calendar; the ICS link works for Apple Calendar, Outlook or anything else.

Checking spots below builds a custom feed combining just those spots into one calendar. Since it's built on the fly rather than being a fixed calendar, it only works as a URL subscription (Google Calendar: Other calendars → From URL; Apple/Outlook: use the link directly) — there's no native one-click "Add" button for a custom mix.

Events show the spot, forecast peak sustained wind and direction. They carry no attendees, no reminders, and are marked as free time. Forecasts update roughly hourly from GFS model data, with a live correction applied where a nearby KNMI weather station is available. Feeds not fetched directly from Google (the ICS links and any custom mix) refresh roughly once a day, on that app's own schedule, not something this site controls.
