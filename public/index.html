Wind Calendar

Kitesurfing wind windows for Dutch spots, pushed straight into a Google Calendar you subscribe to. No account, no app, no sign-in — pick your spots per region below.

How it works

Every spot is checked by default, so Add to Google Calendar adds the whole region in one click if you use Google Calendar (the ICS link works for Apple Calendar, Outlook or anything else). Uncheck any spots you don't care about and the button switches to a custom feed combining just what's checked.

A custom mix can't use the native one-click "Add" button — that only works for a calendar that already exists with fixed content. Instead it's a URL subscription: in Google Calendar use Other calendars → From URL; elsewhere just open the link.

Events show the spot, forecast peak sustained wind and direction. They carry no attendees, no reminders, and are marked as free time. Forecasts update roughly hourly from GFS model data, with a live correction applied where a nearby KNMI weather station is available. A custom mix (and any ICS link) refreshes roughly once a day, on that app's own schedule, not something this site controls.
