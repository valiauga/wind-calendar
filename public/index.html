Wind Calendar

Kitesurfing wind windows for Dutch spots, pushed straight into a Google Calendar you subscribe to. No account, no app, no sign-in — pick your spots below.

    How it works

    Each spot has its own public calendar. Add to Google Calendar adds it directly if you use Google Calendar. For Apple Calendar, Outlook or anything else, use the ICS link instead — those refresh roughly once a day, on that app's own schedule, not something this site controls.

    Events show the spot, forecast peak sustained wind and direction. They carry no attendees, no reminders, and are marked as free time. Forecasts update roughly hourly from GFS model data, with a live correction applied where a nearby KNMI weather station is available.
