Wind Calendar

Kitesurfing wind windows for Dutch spots, pushed straight into a Google Calendar you subscribe to. No account, no app, no sign-in — add a whole region, or just the spots you care about.

How it works

Each region and each individual spot has its own public calendar, so you can subscribe to as many or as few as you like. Add to Google Calendar adds one directly if you use Google Calendar. For Apple Calendar, Outlook or anything else, use the ICS link instead — those refresh roughly once a day, on that app's own schedule, not something this site controls.

Events show the spot, forecast peak sustained wind and direction. They carry no attendees, no reminders, and are marked as free time. Forecasts update roughly hourly from GFS model data, with a live correction applied where a nearby KNMI weather station is available.
